Galactic Overnight Camp

A Unique Astronomy Sleepover Experience

Program Overview

Transform Your school into an interactive space observatory with our unforgettable overnight camp!

Students will:

  • Stargaze through professional telescopes to observe planets, moons and constellations.
  • Sleep in space-themed tents with NASA-style sleeping bags under a "starry" indoor sky(projected constellations).
  • Enjoy space-themed meals: Astronaut breakfast & "Milky Way" dinner included.
